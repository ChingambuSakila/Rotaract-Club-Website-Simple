## 1.2 Purpose and justification

The club needs a professional online home that explains who it is, shows the service it does, and makes it easy for students, Rotarians, sponsors and the public to engage. Today the club's story is scattered across social media posts. A website gives it a stable, searchable and credible presence, and gives the developer a practical learning project in web architecture and design.

## 1.3 Objectives

1. Deliver a responsive five-page website (Home, About Us, Our Impact, Events, Contact & Join Us) by 22 January 2027.
2. Give equal prominence to club identity (mission, leadership, membership) and community service (projects, achievements, measurable impact).
3. Provide a clear route for prospective members and partners to contact the club.
4. Apply the Rotaract cranberry brand consistently, using the colour value confirmed from the club's branding reference.
5. Enable the developer to learn and document the architecture, design principles and implementation concepts used.

## 1.4 Scope

**In scope:** five pages; shared design system (CSS); mobile navigation; contact form with validation; content structure and placeholders; Git/GitHub version control; static hosting and deployment; basic SEO and accessibility; handover notes and documentation.

**Out of scope (for this phase):** backend, database, member login or payments; online event registration with stored data; blog or CMS; multilingual versions; paid advertising. These may be reviewed under change control if requirements justify them.

## 1.8 Assumptions and constraints

**Assumptions:** the club will supply content, logo and photos on time; free static hosting is acceptable; a decision-maker is available within 3 working days for reviews.

**Constraints:** no budget for paid tools; the developer is learning while building; academic commitments and the December break limit available weeks; many visitors will use mobile data, so pages must stay light.

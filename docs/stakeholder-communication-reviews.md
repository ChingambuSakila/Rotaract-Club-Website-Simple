# 3. Stakeholder Communication and Review Sessions

## 3.1 Stakeholder register

| Stakeholder | Interest | Influence | Engagement approach |
|---|---|---|---|
| Club President / Executive | Brand, accuracy, delivery | High | Weekly update; approves milestones |
| Content lead / PR officer | Tone, photos, statistics | High | Weekly check-in; owns content deadlines |
| Club members | Representation, ease of joining | Medium | Surveys and demo sessions |
| Rotarians and district leaders | Accurate Rotary references, credibility | Medium | Review at M2 and M5; launch notice |
| Sponsors and partners | Visibility, trust in impact data | Medium | Preview before launch where relevant |
| Prospective members and public | Clear information, easy contact | Low (direct) | Informal feedback during testing |
| University administration | Appropriate use of the university name | Low | Inform before launch if required |

## 3.2 Communication plan

| Communication | Audience | Frequency | Channel | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Progress update (done, next, blockers, decisions needed) | Sponsor, content lead | Weekly (Friday) | WhatsApp summary or email | Project manager |
| Content request and deadline reminders | Content lead | Weekly until content complete | WhatsApp / email | Project manager |
| Milestone review session | Executive and reviewers | At each milestone | In person or video call | Project manager |
| Change request notice | Sponsor | As needed | Email with change form | Project manager |
| Launch announcement | All stakeholders and public | Once | Social media, email, club meeting | PR officer |
| Post-launch feedback | Members and partners | Once, 2 weeks after launch | Short online form | Project manager |

The agreed rule: questions are answered within 3 working days, and silence after a review request is treated as approval only if the reviewer was told so in writing.

## 3.3 Review sessions

| Review | Target date | Purpose | Attendees | Decision required |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1: Requirements | Week 2 (by 6 Nov) | Confirm SRS, scope, brand colour, content owners | Executive, content lead, Rotary liaison | Approve SRS and scope baseline |
| R2: Design | Week 4 (by 20 Nov) | Review wireframes, colours, typography, tone | Executive, PR officer, 3 to 5 members | Approve design direction |
| R3: Feature-complete demo | Week 7 (by 11 Dec) | Walk through all pages on phone and laptop | Executive, content lead, members | Accept features; list content gaps |
| R4: Release candidate | Week 9 (by 8 Jan) | Content accuracy and final fixes | Executive, content lead, Rotary liaison | Approve for user acceptance |
| R5: UAT and go-live | Week 10 to 11 | Acceptance testing against SRS; launch decision | Sponsor, Executive | Sign off go-live |

## 3.4 Review session format

1. Agenda and links sent 48 hours before (preview link or screenshots).
2. 45-minute session: 10 minutes demo, 25 minutes structured feedback, 10 minutes decisions and actions.
3. Feedback captured in the feedback log (what, page, priority, owner) and triaged within 2 working days into: fix now, schedule, or raise as a change request.
4. Minutes with decisions and actions sent within 24 hours.
5. Sign-off recorded in writing (message or email) and noted in the RTM.

## 3.5 Escalation path

Project manager, then Club President, then the Rotary adviser or club Executive meeting. Escalate when a decision is overdue by more than 5 working days or when the go-live date is at risk.
